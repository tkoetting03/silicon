Curated Reference Compendium: Literature, Music, Cinema, and Fine Art

Thomas Koetting
Department of Electrical Engineering & Computer Science, University of Kansas
Silicon Research Collective, Overland Park
Compendium Aesthetic Canon
Categories 6 Classifications
Media Books / Art / Music
Galleries Louvre / Met / AIC

Abstract — An intellectual framework is shaped through rigorous engagement with arts, humanities, and fundamental sciences. This catalog presents an annotated bibliography of foundational literary texts, acoustic compositions, cinema, classical paintings, architectural monuments, and technical periodicals that serve as primary cultural touchstones for our work.
